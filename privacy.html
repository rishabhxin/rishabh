Your Data Is Safe Here

Welcome! This is my portfolio website, not a data-harvesting laboratory. I respect your privacy the same way I respect clean design and good code.

What I Collect

If you contact me, I might collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email
  • Whatever brilliant idea you type into the message box

I don’t collect your secrets, passwords, browsing history, or favourite midnight snacks.

Why I Collect It

Only for good reasons:

  • To reply to you
  • To talk about work, ideas, or collaborations
  • To be polite and respond like a real human

That’s literally it.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies or analytics tools to see things like:

  • How many people visit
  • Which pages get the most love

No tracking your life. No following you around the internet. Just vibes and numbers.

Third-Party Tools

Some tools help keep this site alive, hosting, forms, analytics, etc. They see limited info and behave responsibly.

What I Don’t Do

  • I don’t sell your data
  • I don’t trade your data
  • I don’t whisper your data to advertisers at night

Your Superpowers

You’re always in control:

  • Don’t want to share info? Totally fine
  • Want your message deleted? Just ask

Changes to This Policy

If I ever update this page, it’ll be because I added something useful, not because I got shady.

Questions?

If you’re curious about privacy, data, or just want to say hi, reach out anytime.

Thanks for trusting this little corner of the internet.

Now go enjoy the rest of the site.

The Eyes

by RP

Published at 05 November, 2026

Manzilein meri ho, bas rahein tumhari ho,
Mushkilein meri ho, bas jeet tumhari ho,
Dhoop mein jalna mera ho, chhaon tumhari ho,
Aansu mere ho, bas aankhein tumhari ho.

Bhook meri ho, bas muskaan tumhari ho,
Raatein meri ho, bas subah tumhari ho,
Qurbaniyan meri ho, bas khushiyan tumhari ho,
Thak kar tootna mera ho, bas umeed tumhari ho.

Umar meri kam ho, bas har dua tumhari ho,
Kismat meri roothe, magar duniya tumhari ho,
Taqdeer meri adhoori rahe, bas kahani tumhari ho,
Maut meri ho, magar har janam mein zindagi tumhari ho.
